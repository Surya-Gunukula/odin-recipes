Overnight Oats

Home

Description

I love overnight oats! They're easy to make-ahead for a quick, on-the-go breakfast. Though this recipe calls for blueberries, you can use almost any fruit. Bananas, peaches, or any variety of berries work best. Top with your favorite nuts or seeds.

Ingredients

  • Milk
  • Yogurt
  • Oats
  • Sugar
  • Honey
  • Chia Seeds
  • Cinnamon
  • Berries

Steps

  1. Combine all the ingredients (besides the fruit) in a jar, seal, and shake.
  2. Open the jar and fold in the fruit.
  3. Seal the jar and refrigerate overnight.
